# Kyrgyz Akylman - Project Design & Development Guidelines

## Visual Identity & Brand Rules
- **Official Brand Colors**: Emerald, green, dark green (`#041a14`, `#05261c`, `#093527`, Emerald-400/500/600, etc.).
- **Gold Color Role**: Gold (`text-amber-300`, `from-amber-400 to-amber-200`, `border-amber-400`) is strictly reserved for **Premium subscription** and all VIP/Premium features. Do NOT use gold for non-premium buttons, generic tools, or standard headers.
- **Background Elements & Photos**: White or black (monochrome / mixed subtle opacity `text-white/20`, `stroke-white/30`, `black/20`) with transparent mathematical meshes.
